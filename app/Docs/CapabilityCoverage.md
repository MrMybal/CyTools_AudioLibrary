## Known gaps a user will notice

1. The taxonomy editor and the device selector were added late: they render, their operations are tested, but nobody clicked them yet.
2. Auto-labelling quality is the model's: probabilities and the responsible model are shown; errors remain.
3. The flat index is exact and in memory: fine for the measured sizes (scale-test.json); an approximate index
   provider would be needed for many millions of segments. The provider interface exists, no ANN provider does.
