Organize and search local sound collections without moving the originals. Browse folders, listen to waveforms, add your own tags and notes, and use compatible audio models for semantic search.

## Getting started {#getting-started}

1. Create a library and choose its cache folder. Add the audio folders you want to reference, then scan the library.
2. Install the analysis engine and the models you want in Installation. Select the models and analyze the library; follow progress in Analysis Queue.
3. Describe a sound in the search bar. Select a result to listen, inspect its matching segment and edit tags. Export a bundle to copy a selection elsewhere.
